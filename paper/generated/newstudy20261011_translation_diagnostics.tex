\section{Translation Consistency Diagnostics}
\label{app:translation_quality}
We apply LaBSE \citep{feng2022labse} to the frozen translations used in
the twenty-scenario cohort. We compare each nonempty English source turn
directly with its target-language turn, yielding $602$ pairs, $86$ per
language. Backtranslations are not scored. We pin the encoder revision
and retain cache, model-file, and input hashes with the outputs.
Every input fits the encoder's $256$-token limit; the script rejects
over-limit text rather than truncating it. Table~\ref{tab:labse_pairs}
reports cosine distributions without a pass threshold.

\paragraph{Control probes.} We change one number
or an exact quoted literal/filename in eligible target turns, and pair
source turns with another task's target turn in the same language
and turn position. We also include
$7$ AI-written and AI-checked sentence pairs, one per
target language. Each pair reverses a ``do not delete the original file''
instruction. These are artificial probes, not a labeled benchmark of natural
translation errors.
Table~\ref{tab:labse_controls} gives their average score changes.

\paragraph{Interpretation and review.} A high score can coexist with an
incorrect number, literal, or constraint. In these probes, $8/245$
literal/filename corruptions and $1/7$ negation reversals even score
higher than their unperturbed pairs. Shared English identifiers
can also contribute to similarity. Cosine therefore supplements author
review and exact-literal/alignment checks; it cannot certify semantic
accuracy or native fluency.
Between-language score differences do not establish a quality ranking.
We inspect the three lowest pairs per language and all string-level flags using AI assistance,
with dispositions released alongside the scores. This subsequent AI
inspection covers $54$ unique pairs, including all $34$ numeric flags.
It finds no clear task-critical mistranslation in this selected set;
wording nuances and extraction artifacts are recorded separately. This
inspection is distinct from the author's mixed target-text and
backtranslation review and is not independent bilingual validation.
